[size=5][b]Overview[/b][/size]
[size=3][b]Secrets of Khazâd-dûm[/b] is a mod expansion for [i]The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria[/i] that restores lost dwarven knowledge, unlocks forgotten forges, and expands how you build, craft, and conquer the darkness beneath the mountain — fully integrated into campaign and sandbox, without altering the main story.[/size]

[size=5][b]Core Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][size=3][b]Armor[/b] — Restored dwarven armor sets (Shayar, Masharuz, Amzul), sandbox-exclusive items now craftable in Campaign, and 3 new BearKnuckle Brawlers gloves.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Construction[/b] — Restored building pieces disabled since v1.2, plus 100+ new pieces (including internal pieces never before available to players).[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Weapons & Materials[/b] — 3 legendary Arms of the Dwarven Kings, the new Crimson Metal Ingot alloy, the Firmaxe Clan Battleaxe, and the BearKnuckle Brawlers Shield.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Farming[/b] — New Nests (Eggs & Feathers), 2 new Lower Deeps Flora cuttings, and farmable Blazeshrooms & Sunken Moss.[/size][/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Expansion I – Kingdoms of Middle Earth[/b][/size]
[size=3]Trade routes reopen and the influence of Men, Elves, and Dwarves spreads through the halls.[/size]
[list]
[*][size=3]New [b]Ceiba Tree[/b] (cutting + wood) from the lands of Arnor.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3]Glittering Caves [b]Pink Opal[/b] & [b]Garnet[/b] lamp collections, via the Rohan Trader.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Viridescent Construction Set[/b] — 30+ Elven/Beorn-inspired pieces (Ceiba Wood), via the Rivendell Trader.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3]Banner sets for [b]Gondor, Rivendell, Lothlórien, Erebor[/b] and [b]Keleariel[/b] (banner + flag + guard post each).[/size][/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Expansion II – Dwarven Traditions[/b][/size]
[size=3]Rediscover the traditions that once brought warmth and pride to Khazâd-dûm.[/size]
[list]
[*][size=3]New [b]Ithildin Doors[/b] (Doors of Durin, Gondor Door, Númenórean Door, +1 planned).[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Cacao Tree[/b] crop and new brews: Anvil Cocoa, Brewed Niphredil Tea, Brewed Elanor Tea, and secret brews.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3]Weapons of the [b]Company of Thorin Oakenshield[/b] (Thorin's Axe & Sword, Balin's Sword & Battleaxe, Dwalin's Battleaxe, Fíli's Warhammer) plus a non-epic Mithril Spear variant.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3]14 secret decorations, secret items and materials, and two new banner sets (commemorative + Firmaxe Clan).[/size][/*]
[*][spoiler][size=3]Hunter's Spear enchanted with the Felbûn rune, which lets you collect trophies from Moria's creatures.[/size][/spoiler][/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Expansion III – Hunter's Lodge[/b][/size]
[size=3]Legendary hunts, creature trophies, and endgame progression, across three parts.[/size]
[list]
[*][size=3][b]Part I – Elemental Drakes:[/b] Fire, Poison and Shadow Drakes (plus a reworked Frost Drake), each with elemental attack debuffs; new hunting materials (drake scales, Nameless Beast Hide, Fell Beast Hide) and matching trophy sets.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Part II – Hunter's Lodge:[/b] Shire-inspired round door & window sets, plus Cave Bat and Snackrat items and trophies.[/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Part III – Legendary Weapons:[/b] the Telchar Hammer (forges legendary weapons), the Mark of Ullr rune, the legendary Ashen Ingot, Tier 7 upgrades for the Royal Dwarven Kings' weapons/Firmaxe Battleaxe/Thorin's Sword, Telchar's knowledge to forge [b]Andúril, Glamdring[/b] and [b]Orcrist[/b], and new legendary weapons (Skeggja of Clan Axbeard, Nakbûr Khazâd, Bûrzul-khuzdâ).[/size][/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[size=3]Unzip the mod's contents into your game's Paks folder:[/size]
[list]
[*][size=3][b]Steam:[/b] [font=Courier New]C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\The Lord of the Rings Return to Moria™\Moria\Content\Paks[/font][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Epic Games:[/b] [font=Courier New]C:\Program Files\Epic Games\ReturnToMoria\Moria\Content\Paks[/font][/size][/*]
[*][size=3][b]Custom install:[/b] find your game folder and follow the same path structure as above.[/size][/*]
[/list]
[size=3][b]Year of the Goat — Porter Goat:[/b] requires [url=https://github.com/jbowensii/MoriaAdvancedBuilder/releases]Mereak Firmaxe's latest pre-release[/url]. Download the installer and follow its instructions.[/size]
