## Secrets Of Khazâd-dûm (Ir-Râruk Khâzad-dûm)
> “Reclaim Moria with the new uncover secrets beneath the mountain

### Overview
**Secrets of Khazâd-dûm** is a mod expansion for _The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria_ that expands the depths of Khazâd-dûm by restoring lost dwarven knowledge, unlocking forgotten forges, and empowering players with new ways to build, craft, and conquer the darkness beneath the mountain.

This expansion focuses on **restored content**, **expanded construction**, and **new dwarven mastery**, seamlessly integrated into the main campaign and sandbox.

### Premise
After the reclaiming of the deeper halls, dwarven scouts uncover sealed archives and abandoned forges once deemed too unstable to be revealed. With renewed mastery of stone and steel, the dwarves of Durin’s Folk begin the great work of rebuilding Khazâd-dûm — stronger, richer, and more glorious than before.
> This mod does not alter the main story outcome but enriches the journey through Moria

## Expansion VI - Coastal Fishing 
> Learn new fishing techniques from your felow dwarves of the Coastal Mountains

### Overview
**Coastal Fishing** is an expansion for **Secrets of Khazâd-dûm**, focused on fishing, resource gathering, and the traditions of the dwarves of the Coastal Mountains.

This expansion introduces fishing resources, specialized traps, a new fishing minigame, and a variety of recipes using the bounty of the waters. Dwarves can now learn to fish, set traps, and make use of creatures found in the water biomes across Khazâd-dûm.

From crabs and shrimp to lobsters, octopuses, and fish, the waters inside the Khazâd-dûm provide new sources of food and materials for those willing to master the art of coastal fishing.

Through new gathering methods and culinary recipes, Coastal Fishing expands the survival experience of Moria while introducing a new way for dwarves to make use of the natural world inside the mountain halls.
### Premise
As the dwarves lightened the beacon in Durin's Tower, they their distant kin from the **Coastal Mountains** started to return to Khazâd-dûm to share their knowledge.

Among these traditions is the art of fishing, a craft developed by dwarves who learned to make their living from the waters surrounding their mountain settlements. Their knowledge of nets, traps, bait, and fishing techniques allowed them to gather food from places where traditional hunting and foraging could not reach.

Now, these techniques are brought back to Khazâd-dûm.

The waters of Khazâd-dûm hold more life than the dwarves once imagined. Crabs hide beneath the shallows, shrimp and lobsters inhabit deeper waters, while fish and other creatures move beneath the surface. With the right tools and knowledge, these creatures can become a valuable source of food for the returning dwarves.

By learning the traditions of the Coastal Mountains, the dwarves can once again make use of the waters around their settlements. Fishing, trapping, and cooking become new parts of dwarven life as the reclaimed kingdom grows beyond its ancient halls.

## Expansion VII - Day of the Dead: Nurtu id-marâd
> A dwarven festival of remembrance honoring the ancestors, preserving the memory of the departed, and celebrating the bonds between generations.

### Overview
**Day of the Dead: Nurtu id-marâd** is a cultural update for **Secrets Of Khâzad-Dûm**, inspired by the traditions of the dwarves of the Coastal Mountains.

This update introduces a unique dwarven festival dedicated to remembering those who have passed away. Through decorated altars, traditional foods, **Lihom Njâm** flowers, sugar skulls, and colorful cloth garlands, the dwarves honor the lives, achievements, and memories of their departed kin.

### Premise
Among the dwarves of the Coastal Mountains, the memory of the departed has always been held sacred. The names of ancestors are preserved, their deeds are recounted, and their craftsmanship lives on in the halls they helped build.

Once every year, on the first full moon after the Dwarven New Year, families gather to celebrate **Nurtu id-marâd**, the Day of the Dead. Homes and halls are adorned with colorful cloth garlands and golden flowers, while carefully arranged altars hold sugar skulls, **memorial bread**, and the favorite foods of those being remembered. Weapons, armor, and portraits are placed among the offerings, serving as reminders of the lives once lived and the legacies left behind.

According to an ancient tradition, the spirits of the departed return during the night to visit their loved ones and partake in the offerings prepared in their honor. Whether this return is a spiritual truth or a cherished belief passed down through generations, the dwarves welcome their ancestors with open hearts and keep their memories alive through celebration.

For the dwarves of the Coastal Mountains, death does not erase one's place among the clan. So long as their names are spoken and their deeds remembered, the departed are never truly forgotten.
